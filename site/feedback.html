Feedback & feature requests

The beta exists to find what's wrong and what's missing before public launch — so if something felt off, or you wanted a check that doesn't exist yet, that's exactly what this is for. Every request gets read; we can't promise every one gets built, but they directly shape what's prioritized next (see the roadmap in the coverage section and plan.md in the repo).

What's useful to include

  • False positive/negative: the rule ID (from findings.json), the file/pattern involved, and why it's wrong.
  • New rule request: the risk you want caught, and — if you have one — a real (or realistic) code example that should trigger it.
  • Language/platform request: which of Python, Java, Kotlin, Swift, Rust, Go, Android, or iOS matters most for what you're building.
  • Anything else: UX friction, confusing report wording, pricing thoughts, whatever got in your way.

What happens next

Fixes and new rules that come out of beta feedback get called out in the changelog so you can see what shipped from what you reported.
