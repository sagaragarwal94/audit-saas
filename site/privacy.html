Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

The short version

The AuditSaas CLI reads your code locally and never sends it anywhere by default. The only thing we collect as a company is what you voluntarily submit through our beta waitlist form (your email and, optionally, a few details about your project) — nothing else, no analytics, no cookies, no tracking scripts on this site.

What the CLI does with your code

When you run saas-audit scan, it reads files on your machine, evaluates them against local detection rules, and writes report files (Markdown/JSON) back into your project directory. No file contents, findings, or metadata are transmitted anywhere unless you explicitly enable the optional AI review feature.

If you do enable AI review (by setting AUDITSAAS_LLM_API_KEY or AUDITSAAS_LLM_BASE_URL), the CLI sends only a compact, redacted summary of findings — not your source code — to the endpoint you configured. That endpoint can be a hosted provider using your own API key, or a fully local model server (Ollama, LM Studio) that never leaves your machine at all. We (AuditSaas) never see this data either way; it goes directly from your machine to the endpoint you chose.

What this website collects

auditsaas.org does not use cookies, analytics, or any tracking scripts. We don't know who visits this site or what pages they view.

The only data we receive is what you submit through the beta waitlist form, hosted by Tally (see below): your email address and any optional answers you provide (project description, tech stack, how you build, feedback). We use this solely to:

  • Decide who to invite to the private beta and in what order
  • Send you an invite, install instructions, and beta-related communication
  • Understand who's interested in AuditSaas and why, to prioritize the product roadmap

We do not sell this data or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party processors

A few services help operate this site and the beta, and each handles data under their own privacy policy:

  • Tally — processes and stores waitlist form submissions.
  • Vercel — hosts this static website.
  • Cloudflare — provides CDN/DNS in front of the site.
  • Your email provider — used to deliver beta invites and communication.

None of these services receive your source code or scan results — those never leave your machine unless you configure AI review yourself, as described above.

Data retention

We keep waitlist submissions for as long as the private beta is running, or until you ask us to delete them. Once AuditSaas has a public launch and pricing, we'll update this policy to cover account and billing data at that point.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete any data we hold about you at any time by emailing beta@auditsaas.org.

Children's privacy

AuditSaas is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page as the product changes, especially around the public launch and the introduction of paid plans (which will involve a payment processor such as Stripe — we will never store your card details ourselves). Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: beta@auditsaas.org.
